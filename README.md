# Gouging Electrodes for Industrial Metal Removal

Gouging electrodes are used in carbon arc gouging applications to remove unwanted metal, prepare weld joints, create bevels, and remove defective weld sections.

Carver Robo Weld offers gouging electrodes for industrial metalworking and fabrication requirements.

## Applications of Gouging Electrodes

Gouging electrodes are used in several industrial processes, including:

- Weld defect removal
- Metal surface preparation
- Joint preparation before rewelding
- Groove and bevel creation
- Industrial repair and maintenance

## Industries and Applications

Carbon arc gouging is used in applications such as:

- Heavy fabrication
- Shipbuilding
- Foundries
- Industrial maintenance
- Metalworking and repair

## Choosing Suitable Gouging Electrodes

The appropriate electrode depends on the base material, required metal removal, electrode dimensions, and equipment compatibility. Always follow the product specifications and recommended operating instructions.
